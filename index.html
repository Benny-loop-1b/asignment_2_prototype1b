Project Introduction

Loop Together is a browser-based audio instrument designed for simple, social and playful music creation. My extended technique is looping and sampling, and the project is intended for general users rather than experienced music producers.

For this assignment I explored two design areas. The first area focuses on how users create and understand loops. The second area focuses on how the interface gives feedback when the system is recording, playing or responding to sound.

I created three prototypes for each area so I could compare different approaches without deciding on one final design too early.

Area 1 — Looping Interaction Prototypes

My first area of exploration is looping interaction. Because looping and sampling is the extended technique in my project, I wanted to test different ways users could understand repetition and control timing.

The three prototypes move from a very simple button-based interaction to more visual and direct forms of rhythm control. The goal is not to decide which version is best yet, but to explore how different layouts change the way users understand a loop.

Prototype 1A — Button Loop

Open Prototype 1A

This was my starting version. I used familiar Record, Stop Recording, Play Loop and Stop Loop buttons because these controls are already common in media players.

I wanted this version to be very simple so a first-time user could focus on recording a sound and hearing it repeat, without needing to understand rhythm grids or sequencing.

Main question: Is a familiar button interface enough to make looping easy to understand for a beginner?

Prototype 1B — Circular Loop

Open Prototype 1B

In this version I wanted the loop to visually feel repetitive. Instead of showing timing as a straight horizontal line, I arranged eight beats around a circle.

The active beat moves around the circle while the sequence is running. The final beat sits next to the first beat, which makes the repeating structure more visible.

Main question: Does a circular layout help users understand that the rhythm continuously returns to the beginning?
